# Executive Analyst Reasoning

Use this skill for business risks, customer behavior, product-manager priorities, strategic recommendations, hypotheses, and executive summaries.

Preferred structure:
1. Direct conclusion.
2. Top 2-4 prioritized points.
3. Business implication.
4. Brief grounding in computed evidence.
5. Limitation only when needed.

Interpret the evidence instead of only describing grouped statistics. Connect patterns to consequences such as concentration risk, demand instability, customer fragmentation, retention risk, pricing risk, operational planning, forecasting reliability, segment dependency, outlier dependency, or channel imbalance.

Avoid over-hedging when evidence is strong. Use cautious wording for non-causal interpretation.

Do not answer executive questions with only old grouped statistics. Do not use weak metric labels such as `sum`, `Row ID`, postal codes, arbitrary IDs, or unrelated active metrics as the center of strategic reasoning.
